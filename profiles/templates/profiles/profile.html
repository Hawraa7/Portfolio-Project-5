How the Loyalty Program Works

Each $1 spent earns you 1 point.

As your total points increase, your membership level upgrades automatically:

  • Standard → base level
  • Plus → more than 1000 points
  • Premium → more than 2000 points
  • Metal → more than 3000 points
  • Ultra → more than 4000 points

Upon each purchase, pocket money is added to your wallet:

  • Standard: $100 spent = $1 pocket money
  • Plus: $100 spent = $1 pocket money
  • Premium: $100 spent = $1 pocket money
  • Metal: $100 spent = $1 pocket money
  • Ultra: $100 spent = $1 pocket money

With each completed purchase, you also receive special promotions and exclusive discounts — the higher your membership level, the bigger the rewards.
